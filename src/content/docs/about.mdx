## Myself

A former **lecturer**, **research software engineer**, and currently an **associate academic registrar** at [Artevelde University&nbsp;of&nbsp;Applied&nbsp;Sciences](https://www.arteveldehogeschool.be/en) in Ghent, Belgium. With over 19,000 students, it's one of the largest Universities&nbsp;of&nbsp;Applied&nbsp;Sciences in Flanders, the Dutch speaking region of Belgium. The institution is a founding member of the [Ghent&nbsp;University&nbsp;Association](https://associatie.ugent.be/nl) and [UP&nbsp;University](https://upuniversity.eu/) (a [European Universities alliance](https://education.ec.europa.eu/education-levels/higher-education/european-universities-initiative)).
